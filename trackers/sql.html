Every question runs against these tables, in your browser. Data changes you make (DELETE, UPDATE, indexes) persist until you click Reset data or reload. Bold columns are primary keys. Timestamps are text in YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS; the reference "today" used by date questions is 2026-09-27.
